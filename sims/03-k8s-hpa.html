Simplifications

  • Only one Resource metric (average CPU utilisation against requests) is modelled. No custom, external or container metrics, and no missing-metrics or not-yet-ready pod adjustments.
  • New pods are assumed ready and reporting metrics instantly; the load is spread evenly over pods and CPU usage can exceed the request (as it can in a real pod without a limit).
  • One sync every 15 seconds (default --horizontal-pod-autoscaler-sync-period). The default scale-up behaviour is applied (at most 100% of current replicas or 4 pods, whichever is larger, per 15 seconds) and scale-down has no rate limit; the scale-up stabilization window is 0.
  • Stabilization follows the documented rule for scale-down: use the highest recommendation from the window. The recommendation history is assumed to begin at the first sync.
  • Load values, request size, targets and replica limits are example numbers.

All numbers shown are examples, not measurements. Keys: Space play/pause, Left back, Right step, R reset.
