Simplifications

  • Only the filters NodeUnschedulable-free flow is modelled: TaintToleration, NodeAffinity (nodeSelector + required), NodeResourcesFit (CPU and memory) and PodTopologySpread (hard, DoNotSchedule, topologyKey zone).
  • Scoring uses NodeResourcesFit (LeastAllocated) and NodeAffinity (preferred, normalised to 0-100) only. The default framework has more score plugins (for example TaintToleration, PodTopologySpread, ImageLocality) which are omitted. Weights shown (1 and 2) are examples.
  • Ties are broken at random by the real scheduler; here the first node wins.
  • No preemption, no scheduling-gate or backoff queues, no percentageOfNodesToScore sampling, no PVC, ports or pod-affinity checks. A Pending pod is not retried.
  • Real events add a trailing preemption sentence that is omitted. Node and pod sizes are example values.

All numbers shown are examples, not measurements. Keys: Space play/pause, Left back, Right step, R reset.
