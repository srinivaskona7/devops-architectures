Quorum arithmetic and etcd sizing

Majority of N = floor(N/2)+1. Failures tolerated = N minus quorum.

NQuorumToleratedNote

Why 3 or 5 and not 4: 4 members need a quorum of 3 and still tolerate only 1 failure, exactly like 3, while adding one more member to run and one more copy to write. A 2/2 split also leaves neither side with a majority. etcd documentation therefore recommends odd cluster sizes, typically 3 or 5; larger clusters add replication cost without being faster.

Simplifications

  • No log compaction or snapshots, no cluster membership change, and no pre-vote or check-quorum (a partitioned leader keeps its role until it sees a higher term, as in the base paper).
  • Time is discrete ticks; election timeout 10 to 19 ticks, heartbeat every 3, message delay 1 tick. All values are examples, not etcd defaults.
  • A new leader appends a no-op entry, as etcd does, so earlier-term entries can be committed (paper section 5.4.2). The walkthrough shortens some timers to demonstrate the vote rule.
  • Reads, linearizability, disk persistence timing and learner nodes are not modelled. Based on Ongaro and Ousterhout, In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm.
