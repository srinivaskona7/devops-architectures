    Simplifications
      

    • All numbers are examples: availability targets, replica counts, error rates and the 5-day synthetic series are illustrative, not measurements of any real service.
      • 

    • Traffic is constant, so the error ratio is the plain mean of 5-minute error rates; real alerts divide error counts by request counts, which weights busy periods more.
      • 

    • Rules are evaluated every 5 minutes with no “for:” delay, no alert resolution or routing, and are not evaluated until the long window has a full set of samples.
      • 

    • The budget counts only the painted series (5 days) inside a 30-day window with no other errors; “time to exhaustion” assumes the current 1-hour burn rate continues.
      • 

    • Composition assumes independent failures (no shared dependencies, no correlated outages, perfect failover). Serial availability = product of availabilities; parallel = 1 − product of (1 − availability).
      • 

    • Only availability SLOs over a fixed 30-day window are covered; rolling vs calendar windows, latency SLOs and error-budget policies are not modelled. Thresholds follow the Workbook table; a strict “greater than” comparison is used.
